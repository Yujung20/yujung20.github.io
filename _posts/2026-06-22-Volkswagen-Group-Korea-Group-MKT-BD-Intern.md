## 공고 원문

Application Closing: Jun 29, 2026

Employment Type: Intern – direct contract with Volkswagen Group Korea

Duration: 6 months from Aug 18, 2026, until the end of Feb 2027

Job Summary:

The incumbent will be responsible for supporting data analytics and reporting, digital projects led by Group Digital team, and to provide administrative assistance to the Group Marketing & Business Development division. This position will report to Group Marketing & Business Development Director.

Main Responsibilities:

Assist managing the customer experience projects.

Update, review and analyze the predefined sales/marketing data set, conduct QA weekly/monthly dashboards in Power BI / Amazon QuickSight and share the result with VWGK stakeholders and brand HQ.

Update user manuals for Power BI / Amazon QuickSight.

Support planning and implementation of internal employee engagement activities (e.g. Townhall, monthly newsletter).

Support planning and implementation of CSR activities conducted via VWGK's non-profit foundation to improve corporate reputation.

Conduct desk research in developing new ideas/proposals. 

Provide adminsitrative support for Group MKT&BD (including CSR)

 

Qualification & Ideal Profile:

Current university students who completed at least 5 semesters or recent graduates within a year
Able to work 6 months from the week of Aug 18, 2026
Excellent communication skills – fluency in Korean & English is essential
Proficient in MS Office
Perform together as one team by treating each other with trust and respect, as well as contributing own skills and perspectives.
Act with commitment and responsibility to achieve high-quality results by continuously focusing on customer needs and company interests.
Able to strive for personal growth and see changes as an opportunity to adopt challenges.
Dare to learn new things & culture and foster future potential.
