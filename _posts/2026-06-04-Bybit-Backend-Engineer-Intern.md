Built on customer-centric values, we endeavour to provide a professional, 24/7 multi-language customer support to help in a timely manner.

Responsibilities

Core Development: Participate in the design and development of backend systems, utilizing Go to build high-performance and high-concurrency microservices.
System Optimization: Gain a deep understanding of business requirements, analyze system bottlenecks, and drive product performance tuning and architectural upgrades.
Platform Engineering: Solve complex technical challenges and contribute to the development and maintenance of common components, middleware, and frameworks.
Cross-functional Collaboration: Work closely with Frontend, QA, and Product teams to ensure high-quality implementation of technical solutions; draft standardized design, technical, and API documentation.
Operations & Monitoring: Monitor live system performance, identify root causes of issues, and provide rapid resolutions for production environment incidents.


Qualifications

Education: 2026 or 2027 graduates. Bachelor’s degree or above in Computer Science, Software Engineering, Communications, or related fields.
Technical Foundations: * Proficiency in Computer Networking (TCP/IP, HTTP, DNS, etc.) and Operating Systems principles (Process/Thread, Memory Management, I/O mechanisms).
Mastery of Data Structures and Algorithms with a commitment to clean, high-quality code.
Technical Proficiency: * Familiarity with Golang and its concurrency models (Goroutines, Channels, Context, etc.).
Proficiency in SQL and familiarity with common databases and storage middleware (e.g., MySQL, Redis).
Soft Skills: * Excellent logical thinking and a strong sense of Ownership; passionate about technology and exploring unknown fields.
Strong bilingual communication skills (English and Chinese) to collaborate effectively in a global team environment.
Internship Requirements: Available for a minimum of 6 months, 5 days per week.
